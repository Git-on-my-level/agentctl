# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The agentctl community aims to be technically rigorous, welcoming, and safe for
people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Participate in good
faith, critique ideas and evidence rather than people, respect stated
boundaries, and make room for clarification and correction.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
intimidation, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing another
person's private information, or pressuring anyone to disclose credentials,
private agent output, or security reports.

## Project spaces

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review
threads, and other spaces where someone is representing the project. Technical
disagreement is expected; personal attacks and retaliatory review are not.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Use GitHub's repository moderation and abuse-reporting tools. If a report
concerns a project maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting route rather than
sending it to that maintainer. Do not use public issues to disclose private
details about the people involved.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, issue warnings,
or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the
community. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, recurrence,
and opportunities to repair harm.

## Scope and changes

Maintainers may clarify this policy as the community grows. Material changes
will be made in the repository history and apply prospectively unless immediate
action is required for safety.
